# The Loom

The engine at the center of [[The Arkadia]], built by [[The Makers]]. It rewrites matter: it can take a dead world apart and weave it back together living. That is the healing.

It also runs the other way. Turned to unmaking, the Loom takes worlds apart and leaves nothing behind. That nothing is [[The Void]].

## Cure and weapon are the same machine
[[Mara's Log 31]] says "Varro thinks the Arkadia is a weapon. It is a cure." Both are true, and that is the point:
- [[Dawn of Arkadia]] and [[The Keeper's Child]] run the Loom forward.
- [[Varro's Crown]] runs it backward. Varro does not corrupt the ship; he uses what it always was.

## Why it is bound to one bloodline
The Makers learned what the Loom could do the hard way ([[The Unmaking]]). Afterwards [[The Last Captain]] bound the ship so that only their own line, carrying their key, could ever wake it again ([[Access Key]], [[The Heart and the Helm]]).

## Why it needs a keeper at the heart
Unmaking leaves a wound that wants to keep spreading. Someone at the heart has to hold that wound, [[The Rift]], shut at all times, whether the ship is dark or awake. The Loom can only heal while someone is there, so the keeper is the cost of every healing.
