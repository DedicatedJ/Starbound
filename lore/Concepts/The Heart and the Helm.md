# The Heart and the Helm

The rule at the center of [[The Arkadia]]: it takes two people to heal the galaxy.

| Station | What it does | Who can take it |
|---|---|---|
| **The heart** (the core) | Holds [[The Rift]] shut while the ship works. Whoever is there is sealed in, half asleep, for as long as the ship heals. | Anyone willing |
| **The helm** | Wakes the ship, and sets and changes its course | Waking: only the captain's bloodline carrying the [[Access Key]]. Steering an awake ship: anyone |

## Consequences
- **Why ten years passed with nothing healed.** [[Mara Solis]] arrived alone. Holding the heart stopped the [[Void Collapse]] from spreading, but with no one at the helm the ship never woke.
- **Why Mara split the map** ([[Map Fragments]]): the second keeper had to be someone she chose, and a Solis.
- **Why [[Captain Varro]] needs the player:** he can steer the ship, but he cannot wake it or open the vaults.
- **The three endings** are the three ways to fill the two stations: [[Dawn of Arkadia]] (player at the helm, Mara at the heart), [[The Keeper's Child]] (Mara wakes it, player at the heart), [[Varro's Crown]] (player wakes it, Varro takes the helm).
- **Once awake, the ship holds its course by itself.** The helm only matters for waking it and for changing course, which is why Mara can go home in The Keeper's Child.
