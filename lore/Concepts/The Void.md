# The Void

Whatever swallowed the dead worlds. Its spread is the [[Void Collapse]]; its wound is [[The Rift]]. [[The Arkadia]] can push it back. [[Void Shielding]] protects against it; the [[Void Beam]] somehow uses it.

## What it is (draft)
Unmade space: what is left when [[The Loom]] takes a world apart and weaves nothing back. It is a wound, not a force with a will, but it spreads unless someone holds it shut.

It first opened in [[The Unmaking]], was held shut by [[The First Keeper]] for ages, and broke loose again as the [[Void Collapse]] when that keeper died.

The [[Void Beam]] and [[Void Shielding]] are both made from the Loom's remains ([[Alien Essence]] explains the shielding).
