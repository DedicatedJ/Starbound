# Void Collapse

The disaster that swallowed half the settled worlds before the game begins. [[The Rift]] is its scar. [[Mara Solis]] left to find a way to stop it.

## Where it stands
The Collapse stopped spreading ten years ago, when [[Mara Solis]] took the heart of [[The Arkadia]] and held [[The Rift]] shut. The dead worlds stayed dead and the survivors scraped by. Now she is weakening and the Rift is widening again, so the game takes place on the edge of a second Collapse.

## What caused it
Not a natural disaster. One generation before the game, [[The First Keeper]], who had held [[The Rift]] shut since [[The Unmaking]], finally died. With the heart empty, the old wound broke open and began swallowing worlds. Ten years ago [[Mara Solis]] took the heart and stopped it. Now she is failing.
