# Map Fragments

The three pieces of [[The Arkadia]]'s coordinates that [[Mara Solis]] cut from her [[Star Map]] and sealed in vaults.

| Fragment | Vault | Log |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Cinder Vault]] | [[Mara's Log 31]] |
| 2 | [[Glacius Vault]] | [[Mara's Log 44]] |
| 3 | [[Verdant Vault]] | [[Mara's Log 52]] |

With all three, [[Quill]] reveals the truth about Mara, and [[The Rift]] can be entered.
