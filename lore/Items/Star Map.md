# Star Map

[[Mara Solis]]'s map of the sector: [[Kepler Reach]], [[Drift Station]], [[Cinder]], [[Glacius]], [[Verdant Deep]] and [[The Rift]]. Incomplete on purpose: the route to [[The Arkadia]] is split into three [[Map Fragments]].

The game's title, *The Last Starchart*, refers to this map.
