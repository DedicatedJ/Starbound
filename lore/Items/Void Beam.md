# Void Beam

A piercing beam weapon, one of the player's five weapons. Its name ties it to [[The Void]], but no lore explains it yet.

Status is `idea`: it exists in the game as a mechanic only.

## What it is (draft)
A fragment of [[The Loom]]'s unmaking, salvaged from a Maker wreck and built into a gun. It does not burn; it takes apart whatever it touches, which is why it passes through several targets. [[Varro's Crews]] carry them, and the player can too.

It is the game's first quiet hint that the same power that heals can unmake.
