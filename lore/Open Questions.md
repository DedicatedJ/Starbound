# Open Questions

Holes the current lore does not answer. Each one links to the notes it affects. Answer a question by writing the answer into those notes, then delete it here.

## Characters
1. **What is the player's name?** Quill calls them "little Solis" and Varro "the Solis brat". Gender, age and where they lived for ten years are all unknown. ([[The Player]])
2. **Why did Mara leave without saying goodbye?** [[Mara's Log 44]] apologizes for it but never explains. ([[Mara Solis]])
3. **Who is the player's other parent?** Never mentioned. ([[The Player]], [[Mara Solis]])
4. **What is Quill, really?** Described as "an old drone". Is Quill related to the hostile [[Drones]]? Who built Quill, and why is it loyal to Mara? ([[Quill]])
5. **How does Varro know about the Arkadia and the Solis family?** He knows the player by name and expects them to find coordinates. ([[Captain Varro]])

## The world
6. **What caused the Void Collapse?** Natural disaster, experiment, attack? ([[Void Collapse]], [[The Void]])
7. **Where are the other settled worlds?** The map shows six locations; half the settled worlds were lost. Where do people live now? ([[Void Collapse]])
8. **Who built the Arkadia?** The access key confirms "Solis lineage". Did the Solis family build it, or did it choose them? ([[The Arkadia]])
9. **Who controls the drones in the Rift?** The Arkadia's own defenses, something from the Void, or something else? ([[Drones]], [[The Rift]])
10. **Who built the vaults?** Did Mara build three vaults with puzzles, or did she hide fragments in older ruins with glyphs on the walls? ([[Cinder Vault]], [[Glacius Vault]], [[Verdant Vault]])
11. **Whose survey team vanished on Verdant Deep, and when?** ([[The Lost Survey Team]])
12. **What is alien essence?** It is a currency for upgrades, but what is it made of and why does it drop from creatures? ([[Alien Essence]])
