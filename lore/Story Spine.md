# Story Spine

The whole story in order, with the plot holes closed. Everything already in the game still holds; this fills the gaps between those facts. Each step links to the notes that carry the detail. What the game itself needs to say differently is in [[Game Changes Needed]].

## Before the game
0. **The Unmaking.** The Makers' great ship, [[The Arkadia]], carries [[The Loom]], which weaves dead worlds back to life. Some of them turn it to unmaking in a war, and the wound they tear becomes [[The Void]]. [[The Last Captain]] takes the helm back and [[The First Keeper]] holds the wound shut from the heart. The Makers do not recover. ([[The Unmaking]])
1. **The Makers build the Arkadia.** A ship that can heal dead worlds, and a chain of vaults across the sector marked in their glyphs. The ship is bound to its last captain's bloodline: only that line, carrying the captain's key, can wake it. Then the Makers vanish. ([[The Makers]], [[The Arkadia]])
2. **The key becomes an heirloom.** The Solis family carries it for generations without knowing what it opens. ([[Access Key]])
3. **The Void Collapse.** After ages, [[The First Keeper]] dies. The heart is empty, the old wound breaks open, and half the settled worlds go dark, [[Corvane]] among the first. [[Quill]], the keeper's attendant, drifts out of [[The Rift]] broken. ([[Void Collapse]])
4. **Mara finds Quill.** Exploring the Rift's edge, [[Mara Solis]] recovers a broken drone drifting out of it: [[Quill]], one of the Arkadia's own drones, its memory mostly gone. Quill remembers the ship, the vaults, and the shape of the key. Mara realizes the myth is real. Later, Quill recognizes the heirloom around her neck as the key ([[Mara's Log 7]]).
5. **Varro gives chase.** Soon after she leaves, [[Captain Varro]], who lost [[Corvane]] and gathered its survivors into [[Varro's Crews]], hears she is hunting a ship that can remake worlds. He offers to partner; she refuses ([[Mara's Log 18]]). He follows her.
6. **Mara hides her child.** [[Tamsin Hale]] raises [[The Player]] under another name on a quiet world off her map. Mara leaves without saying goodbye so that no trail leads back. ([[Mara's Log 1]], [[Mara's Log 44]])
7. **Mara splits the map.** She finds the Makers' last starchart in a derelict ([[Mara's Log 26]]), learns their history from the [[Glacius]] crystals ([[Mara's Log 38]]), copies the Arkadia's position, cuts them into three [[Map Fragments]], and re-tunes three of the Makers' vaults so that their doors open only for the key. Varro watches her open the [[Cinder Vault]] and learns the one thing that matters: only a Solis can open those doors. He claims the vault and settles in to wait. ([[Mara's Log 31]], [[Mara's Log 52]])
8. **Mara reaches the Arkadia, alone.** She learns its rule, [[The Heart and the Helm]]: one person at the heart holds the Rift shut, a second at the helm wakes the ship and steers its healing. Alone, she chooses the heart. The Collapse stops spreading. Nothing heals. This is ten years before the game.
9. **Quill keeps a promise.** Quill runs [[Drift Station]] as Mara asked: do not follow me, do not lead anyone to my child, and if my ship ever comes home, guide whoever it brings. Quill cannot open the vaults or survive the Rift, so it waits.

## The game begins
10. **Mara's strength fails.** After ten years the Rift is widening again. From the heart, Mara launches [[Mara's Ship]] home, empty, carrying the key and her [[Star Map]], and records a final message ([[Mara's Final Recording]]). Only three words make it through the Void: *Find the Arkadia*.
11. **The ship finds her child.** Its courier drone finds the player; the ship sets them down on [[Kepler Reach]], Mara's first camp, out of Varro's sight, where it waits for them to learn to survive.
12. **Quill, Varro and the vaults.** Quill sends for them. Varro offers his deal, because he needs a Solis to open the vaults and wake the ship. With the key, the player opens all three vaults.
13. **The truth.** With all three fragments, Quill tells them Mara is alive in the heart, and that she is failing.
14. **The Rift.** The Arkadia's [[Drones]] still follow their last standing order, keep everyone away, and the sleeping ship cannot call them off until the key docks. If the player refused Varro, his fleet shadows them to the Rift's edge, waiting to take the ship once it wakes.

## The choice
15. **[[Dawn of Arkadia]]:** the player takes the helm and wakes the ship. Mara stays at the heart, as she chose ten years ago.
16. **[[The Keeper's Child]]:** the player frees Mara. She wakes the ship from the helm, then the player takes her place at the heart and Quill flies her home.
17. **[[Varro's Crown]]:** the player wakes the ship and hands Varro the helm. Steering does not need a Solis, only waking does, so he can turn it from healing to burning.
