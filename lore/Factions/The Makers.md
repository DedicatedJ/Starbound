# The Makers

Whoever built [[The Arkadia]] and the vaults. Nothing in the game names them yet.

## What we know (draft)
- They built the Arkadia to heal worlds lost to [[The Void]], which suggests the Void came before.
- They left vaults across the sector, waystations marked with glyphs: the [[Cinder Vault]], [[Glacius Vault]] and [[Verdant Vault]]. The wall hints in the game are their glyphs.
- They bound the Arkadia to its last captain's bloodline and key; that line became the Solis family ([[Access Key]]).
- [[Quill]] and the Rift [[Drones]] are their machines.
- They are gone.

## Open
Who they were, what happened to them, whether the Void destroyed them, and whether anything of them survives (the [[Glacius]] crystals that "remember sound" are a good place to hide an answer).
