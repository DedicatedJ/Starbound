# Varro's Crews

The pirates under [[Captain Varro]]. They hold [[Cinder]] and roam desert and ice worlds.

## What we know
- They followed [[Mara Solis]] to Cinder.
- If [[The Player]] accepts Varro's deal, the crews stand down and only fight if attacked first.
- If the player refuses, they fight on every world.
- In [[Varro's Crown]], Varro's fleet takes [[The Arkadia]] and conquers the galaxy.

## In the game
Space pirates: armed humans who patrol, chase and shoot. They drop salvage.
