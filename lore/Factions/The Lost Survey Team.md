# The Lost Survey Team

A survey team swallowed by the jungle of [[Verdant Deep]]. Mentioned once, in the planet's description.

## What happened
Twelve surveyors from the old colonial authority, sent to [[Verdant Deep]] early in the [[Void Collapse]] to look for a way to stop it. They found the [[Verdant Vault]] and could not open it: the Makers' doors had been sealed and dead since [[The Unmaking]]. Years later [[Mara Solis]]'s [[Access Key]] woke them, and she re-tuned them to open only for the key. The jungle took them: no bodies, twelve packs, twelve bunks. [[Mara Solis]] found their camp ([[Mara's Log 47]]), and their notes are the first record of what [[Alien Essence]] is.

Their camp is still out there for the player to find.
