# Tamsin Hale

A retired spacer and [[Mara Solis]]'s oldest friend, who raised [[The Player]] under another name after Mara left ([[Mara's Log 1]]).

## Story (draft)
Tamsin knew only that Mara was going somewhere dangerous and that her child must not be found. She raised the player on a quiet farming world off the [[Star Map]], told them their mother was a spacer who died, and never said the name Solis.

The courier drone found the player at Tamsin's door. Whether Tamsin is still alive, and what she knew, is open.
