# Quill

An old drone who runs [[Drift Station]]. Kept the station running for [[Mara Solis]] and kept her secrets.

## What we know
- Sends the first message to [[The Player]] after the tutorial: "Come and see me, little Solis."
- Explains that the [[Star Map]] is incomplete on purpose and that the [[Map Fragments]] are in three vaults.
- Runs the terminal that sells upgrades for salvage and [[Alien Essence]] ([[Jump Drive Mk II]], [[Void Shielding]], gear).
- Promised Mara to keep one truth until the player had all three fragments: Mara did not die; she is holding the Rift shut from inside [[The Arkadia]].
- In [[The Keeper's Child]], Quill flies Mara home.

## Personality
Patient, formal, fond of the player. Speaks in short, kind sentences. Guides without pushing.
