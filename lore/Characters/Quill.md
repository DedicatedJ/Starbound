# Quill

An old drone who runs [[Drift Station]]. Kept the station running for [[Mara Solis]] and kept her secrets.

## What we know
- Sends the first message to [[The Player]] after the tutorial: "Come and see me, little Solis."
- Explains that the [[Star Map]] is incomplete on purpose and that the [[Map Fragments]] are in three vaults.
- Runs the terminal that sells upgrades for salvage and [[Alien Essence]] ([[Jump Drive Mk II]], [[Void Shielding]], gear).
- Promised Mara to keep one truth until the player had all three fragments: Mara did not die; she is holding the Rift shut from inside [[The Arkadia]].
- In [[The Keeper's Child]], Quill flies Mara home.

## Personality
Patient, formal, fond of the player. Speaks in short, kind sentences. Guides without pushing.

## Origin (draft)
Quill is one of [[The Arkadia]]'s own drones, built by [[The Makers]]. It drifted out of the Rift broken, its memory mostly gone, and [[Mara Solis]] found it. What it remembered (the ship, the vaults, the shape of the key) is how Mara learned the Arkadia was real. Quill has been loyal to her ever since.

It is the same kind of machine as the hostile Rift [[Drones]]; the difference is that Quill woke up, and they never did.

## Why Quill waited ten years
Mara's orders: do not follow me, do not lead anyone to my child, and if my ship ever comes home, guide whoever it brings. Quill also could not have done more: the vaults open only for the Solis key, and an old drone would not survive the Rift.

## What Quill remembers (draft)
- Quill was the attendant of [[The First Keeper]], tending them in [[The Arkadia]]'s heart for ages.
- It was there when the First Keeper died, the moment the [[Void Collapse]] began. Quill has never said this out loud. It blames itself for not being able to hold the door.
- It remembers [[The Last Captain]]'s face. "You have her eyes" may mean more than Mara.
- The Rift [[Drones]] are its siblings: the same make, still following orders it woke up from.

## Its conflict
Quill's oldest duty is to the ship and the heart. Helping the player reach the Arkadia means helping them toward the fate it watched the First Keeper suffer, and that Mara is suffering now. It helps anyway, because Mara asked. In [[The Keeper's Child]], flying Mara home is the first time in its long life that Quill gets someone out of the heart.
