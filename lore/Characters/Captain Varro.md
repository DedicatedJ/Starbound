# Captain Varro

Pirate captain who leads [[Varro's Crews]]. Claims [[Cinder]] as his: "my rock, my crews, my vault".

## What we know
- Contacts [[The Player]] on Cinder and offers a deal: share the Arkadia's coordinates and his crews will leave them alone everywhere.
- If refused, his crews fight the player on every world.
- [[Mara Solis]] wrote that he thinks [[The Arkadia]] is a weapon ([[Mara's Log 31]]). He followed her to Cinder.
- If the player made the deal, they can hand him the Arkadia, which leads to [[Varro's Crown]]: he turns its engines from healing to burning and rules the galaxy.

## Personality
Blunt, mocking, transactional. Keeps his word when it profits him ("My crews will not touch you unless you start it").
