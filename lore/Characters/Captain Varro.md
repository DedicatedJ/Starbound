# Captain Varro

Pirate captain who leads [[Varro's Crews]]. Claims [[Cinder]] as his: "my rock, my crews, my vault".

## What we know
- Contacts [[The Player]] on Cinder and offers a deal: share the Arkadia's coordinates and his crews will leave them alone everywhere.
- If refused, his crews fight the player on every world.
- [[Mara Solis]] wrote that he thinks [[The Arkadia]] is a weapon ([[Mara's Log 31]]). He followed her to Cinder.
- If the player made the deal, they can hand him the Arkadia, which leads to [[Varro's Crown]]: he turns its engines from healing to burning and rules the galaxy.

## Personality
Blunt, mocking, transactional. Keeps his word when it profits him ("My crews will not touch you unless you start it").

## What he knows, and why he wants the player (draft)
He followed [[Mara Solis]] to [[Cinder]] and watched her open the vault. That taught him the rule: only a Solis with the key can open those doors, and only a Solis can wake the ship. He cannot open "his" vault, so he took the planet and waited ten years for another Solis to show up.

His deal is honest about what he wants: the coordinates, and later the ship. He can steer [[The Arkadia]] once it is awake, but he needs the player to wake it ([[The Heart and the Helm]]).

## If the player refuses
He does not give up. His fleet shadows the player to the edge of [[The Rift]], planning to take the ship once it wakes. In [[Dawn of Arkadia]] and [[The Keeper's Child]], the waking Arkadia scatters his fleet. (Not in the game yet; see [[Game Changes Needed]].)
