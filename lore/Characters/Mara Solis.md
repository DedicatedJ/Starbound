# Mara Solis

An explorer and the mother of [[The Player]]. Ten years before the game she flew into the dark looking for a way to stop the [[Void Collapse]] and never came back.

## What we know
- She believed [[The Arkadia]] was real, and that it is a cure, not a weapon ([[Mara's Log 31]]).
- She split the Arkadia's coordinates into three [[Map Fragments]] and sealed them in vaults on [[Cinder]], [[Glacius]] and [[Verdant Deep]], leaving a log with each.
- She trusted [[Quill]] with her secrets, including the truth about what she planned to do.
- She reached the Arkadia, found it could not heal the galaxy unless someone held [[The Rift]] shut from its core, and took that place herself ([[Mara's Final Recording]]).
- She may still be alive inside the core.
- Her ship, [[Mara's Ship]], and her [[Star Map]] were delivered to her child at the start of the game.

## Personality
Driven, secretive, protective. She hides things "right under their noses" and tunes puzzles "so only someone patient could open it". She is sorry she left without saying goodbye ([[Mara's Log 44]]).

## Relationships
- [[The Player]]: her child
- [[Quill]]: keeper of her station and her secrets
- [[Captain Varro]]: pursued her; thinks the Arkadia is a weapon
