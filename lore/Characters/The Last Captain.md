# The Last Captain

The last captain of [[The Arkadia]], and the ancestor of the Solis family.

## Story
During [[The Unmaking]] they took the helm back from the Makers who had turned the ship into a weapon. Then they bound the ship to their own bloodline and key, left [[The First Keeper]] at the heart, and walked away with the [[Access Key]] so no one could ever wake the ship without their line.

Their descendants kept the key as an heirloom and forgot what it opened, until [[Mara Solis]].

[[Quill]] remembers their face.
