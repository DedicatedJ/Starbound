# The Player

The child of [[Mara Solis]]. Unnamed so far. Quill calls them "little Solis"; [[Captain Varro]] calls them "the Solis brat". Quill says they have Mara's eyes.

## What we know
- A courier drone delivered the [[Access Key]] to [[Mara's Ship]], her [[Star Map]] and the words *Find the Arkadia*.
- Starts the game on [[Kepler Reach]] with no combat experience.
- Varro says they "finally crawled out of hiding", so they may have been hiding for the ten years Mara was gone.
- The Arkadia recognizes their "Solis lineage" and lets them aboard.
- Their final choice decides the ending: [[Dawn of Arkadia]], [[The Keeper's Child]] or [[Varro's Crown]].
