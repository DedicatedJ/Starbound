# Mara's Logs

[[Mara Solis]] kept a numbered log from the night she left to the day she reached [[The Arkadia]]. The game has three of them, numbered 31, 44 and 52, so about fifty exist. The rest are where the deeper story can live, found in the world rather than told.

## The arc

| Logs | Where she is | What they carry |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 10 | Leaving; [[Drift Station]] | The night she left ([[Mara's Log 1]]), what [[Quill]] remembers ([[Mara's Log 7]]), the vaults on Quill's broken map |
| 11 to 25 | Hunting along the Rift's edge | [[Captain Varro]] finds her and offers to partner ([[Mara's Log 18]]); she refuses; the chase begins |
| 26 to 30 | A Maker derelict at the Rift's edge | She finds the Makers' last starchart and copies the Arkadia's position from it ([[Mara's Log 26]]) |
| 31 to 37 | [[Cinder]] | Hides the first fragment under Varro's nose ([[Mara's Log 31]], in the game) |
| 38 to 44 | [[Glacius]] | The crystals play the Makers' last voices: she learns about [[The Loom]] and [[The Unmaking]] ([[Mara's Log 38]]); her apology ([[Mara's Log 44]], in the game) |
| 45 to 52 | [[Verdant Deep]] | She finds [[The Lost Survey Team]]'s camp ([[Mara's Log 47]]); the last fragment and her decision ([[Mara's Log 52]], in the game) |

## How the game could use them
Logs found as pickups in the open worlds, a few per planet, readable from the pause menu. Each one is a short entry, and together they tell Mara's side of the story. Not in the game yet.
