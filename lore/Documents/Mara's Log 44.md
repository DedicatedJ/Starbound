Found with fragment 2 in the [[Glacius Vault]] on [[Glacius]]. Written by [[Mara Solis]].

> The crystals here remember sound. I tuned the vault so only someone patient could open it. If that is you: I am sorry I left without saying goodbye.

## What it reveals
- The crystals of [[Glacius]] remember sound.
- Mara expected her child to follow, and regrets leaving.
