# Mara's Log 38

**Where:** Glacius. Part of [[Mara's Logs]]. Not in the game yet.

> The crystals sang to me tonight. They are not crystals. They are a record, and the voices in them are the Makers, at the end. They built a loom that could weave dead worlds back to life, and then some of them turned it around. The Void is not a storm. It is a wound, and they made it. Varro was half right. That is the worst part.

## What it reveals
The Makers' history: [[The Loom]], [[The Unmaking]], and that cure and weapon are the same machine.
