# Mara's Log 1

**Where:** Drift Station, the night she left. Part of [[Mara's Logs]]. Not in the game yet.

> I did not say goodbye. If I had, I would have stayed, and if I had stayed, whoever else is hunting this ship would have followed me home one day and found my child at the door. Better that the child grows up angry at a mother who left than grows up hunted. Tamsin will keep them safe under the new name. Quill will keep the station. I will keep going.

## What it reveals
Why Mara left without a word, and that the player was hidden under another name with [[Tamsin Hale]].
