# Mara's Log 7

**Where:** Drift Station. Part of [[Mara's Logs]]. Not in the game yet.

> Quill remembered something tonight. Not words, a shape: a door in a cliff on a burning world, a door in ice, a door in green. It drew them for me with a welding torch on the hangar floor. Then it stopped, and looked at the key around my neck for a very long time, and asked me where I got it.

## What it reveals
Quill's memory of the vaults, and the first hint that the Solis heirloom is the [[Access Key]].
