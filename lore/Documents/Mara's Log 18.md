# Mara's Log 18

**Where:** Rift's edge. Part of [[Mara's Logs]]. Not in the game yet.

> Varro came alone, which I did not expect. He told me about Corvane. He told me what the core worlds did, or did not do. He wants the ship so that it never happens again, he says, and I believe that he believes it. I told him no. He said he would be patient. I think that is the most frightening thing anyone has ever said to me.
