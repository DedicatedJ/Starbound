# Mara's Final Recording

Played when [[The Player]] boards [[The Arkadia]].

> If you are hearing this, you found it. The Arkadia can heal every world the Void took, but someone has to stay at its heart and hold the Rift shut while it works.
>
> I have held it as long as I could. The choice is yours now.

## The choice
- [[Dawn of Arkadia]]: wake the Arkadia and heal the galaxy (always available)
- [[The Keeper's Child]]: free Mara and take her place (needs Quill's truth)
- [[Varro's Crown]]: hand the Arkadia to Varro (needs the deal with [[Captain Varro]])
