# Mara's Log 26

**Where:** A Maker derelict at the Rift's edge. Part of [[Mara's Logs]]. Not in the game yet.

> The last starchart. The Makers left it in a dead ship like a note on a table: every world they ever healed, and one bright point in the middle of the dark where their great ship went to sleep. I copied it by hand. Then I cut the copy into pieces, because if I can find this, so can he.

## What it reveals
Where Mara's [[Star Map]] came from: the Makers' last starchart. This is the game's title.
