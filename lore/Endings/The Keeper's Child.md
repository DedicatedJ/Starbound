# The Keeper's Child

**Choice:** Free Mara from the core and take her place.
**Requires:** hearing the truth from [[Quill]] (flag `mara_alive_clue`)

The player opens the core and Mara stumbles out, older, thinner and alive. Then the player takes her place, holding the Rift shut while the Arkadia heals the galaxy. Quill flies Mara home; she visits every year.

## Consequences
- The galaxy heals.
- [[Mara Solis]] is free; [[The Player]] becomes the keeper.
