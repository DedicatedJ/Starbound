# Dawn of Arkadia

**Choice:** Wake the Arkadia and heal the galaxy.
**Requires:** nothing; always available

The Arkadia wakes and the Rift folds shut behind it. World by world, the dead planets turn green. Mara sleeps on in the core, holding the last of the Void at bay, and the player stands at the helm she left them.

## Consequences
- The galaxy heals.
- [[Mara Solis]] stays in the core.

## How it works (draft)
The player takes the helm; Mara stays at the heart, the station she chose ten years ago ([[The Heart and the Helm]]). It is bittersweet on purpose: the galaxy heals and she does not come home. If the player refused [[Captain Varro]], his fleet waiting at the Rift's edge scatters when the ship wakes.
