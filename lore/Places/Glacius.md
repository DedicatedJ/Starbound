# Glacius

A frozen world with ringing crystal pylons. The crystals here "remember sound" ([[Mara's Log 44]]).

## What happens here
The [[Glacius Vault]] holds the second of the [[Map Fragments]]. Reaching Glacius needs the [[Jump Drive Mk II]].

## Inhabitants
Pirates from [[Varro's Crews]].
