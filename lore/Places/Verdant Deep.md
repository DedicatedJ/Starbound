# Verdant Deep

A deep jungle that swallowed [[The Lost Survey Team]]. The last and hardest vault world.

## What happens here
The [[Verdant Vault]] holds the third of the [[Map Fragments]] and [[Mara's Log 52]]. Reaching it needs the [[Jump Drive Mk II]].

## Inhabitants
[[Alien Wildlife]] in the jungle; pirates on the ice.
