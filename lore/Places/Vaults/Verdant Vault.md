# Verdant Vault

The vault on [[Verdant Deep]]. Guards the third of the [[Map Fragments]], left by [[Mara Solis]] with [[Mara's Log 52]].

## The puzzle
Pressure plates: numbered plates toggle when stood on; the active plates must add up to the number on the door.

Wall glyph: "the stones must weigh exactly what the door asks."

## Lore
The last fragment. Mara wrote her final log here, already planning to hold the Rift shut herself.
