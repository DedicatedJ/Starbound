# Cinder Vault

The vault on [[Cinder]]. Guards the first of the [[Map Fragments]], left by [[Mara Solis]] with [[Mara's Log 31]].

## The puzzle
Pylon memory: colored pylons light up in a sequence; the player activates them in the same order.

Wall glyph: "watch the pylons, then wake them in the same order."

## Lore
Mara hid this fragment right under the pirates' noses. [[Captain Varro]] calls it "my vault" without knowing what is inside.
