# Glacius Vault

The vault on [[Glacius]]. Guards the second of the [[Map Fragments]], left by [[Mara Solis]] with [[Mara's Log 44]].

## The puzzle
Resonating crystals: shooting a crystal toggles it and its two neighbours; the door opens when every crystal sings.

Wall glyph: "strike the crystals until every one of them sings."

## Lore
The crystals of Glacius remember sound. Mara tuned this vault so only someone patient could open it.
