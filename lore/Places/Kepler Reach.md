# Kepler Reach

Where [[The Player]] sets down in [[Mara's Ship]]. Overgrown and quiet, mostly. Jungle and desert.

## What happens here
- The tutorial: the player learns to move, shoot and survive against [[Alien Wildlife]].
- After the tutorial, the [[Ship Computer]] relays [[Quill]]'s message.

## Inhabitants
[[Alien Wildlife]] in the jungle; pirates from [[Varro's Crews]] in the desert.
