# The Arkadia

A huge, ancient ship that can bring dead planets back to life and push [[The Void]] back. Spacers tell stories about it; [[Mara Solis]]'s [[Star Map]] says it is real.

## What we know
- It drifts inside [[The Rift]], guarded by [[Drones]].
- It recognizes the [[Access Key]] and "Solis lineage", and speaks when the player boards.
- It can heal every world the Void took, but someone must stay at its heart and hold the Rift shut. The heart holds it at all times, whether the ship is dark or awake, and the ship can only heal while someone is there.
- Mara is in its core, doing exactly that.
- [[Captain Varro]] believes it is a weapon. In [[Varro's Crown]] he proves it can be used as one.

## Filed here because
It is something the player travels to and boards. It is also an artifact and a voice; link here from those contexts instead of making new notes.

## How it works
- Built by [[The Makers]] and bound to its last captain's bloodline, which became the Solis family.
- It needs two keepers: [[The Heart and the Helm]]. One at the heart holds the Rift shut; one at the helm wakes it and steers its healing.
- Waking it takes a Solis with the [[Access Key]]. Once awake, it holds its course on its own, and anyone at the helm can change that course, which is how [[Captain Varro]] turns it into a weapon.
- Asleep, it cannot call off its [[Drones]]. They keep everyone away, including the heir, until the key docks.
- Mara has been at its heart for ten years. With no one at the helm, it has not healed anything.

## History
The Makers' great ship of making, built around [[The Loom]]. In [[The Unmaking]] it was used as a weapon. [[The Last Captain]] took it back and bound it to their line; [[The First Keeper]] held its heart for ages. The keeper's death began the [[Void Collapse]]; Mara took the empty heart ten years ago.
