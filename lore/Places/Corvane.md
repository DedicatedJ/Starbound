# Corvane

[[Captain Varro]]'s home world. Not on [[Star Map]]: it is gone, one of the first worlds swallowed by the [[Void Collapse]].

## Story
Varro was a young officer in Corvane's defense fleet when the Void reached it. The core worlds promised evacuation ships that never came. He got a few thousand people off on what he could steal. Everyone else died watching the sky come apart.

The survivors became the first of [[Varro's Crews]].

## Open
Whether anything of Corvane is left at the Rift's edge, and whether Varro ever goes back.
