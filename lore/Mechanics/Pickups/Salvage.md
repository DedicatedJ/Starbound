# Salvage

Scrap from pirates and drones. Spent at [[Drift Station]] on upgrades.

`value` is the amount restored (or damage added, or trade value); `lifetime` is how many seconds it stays on the ground. Drop chances live on each enemy's note (`drop_salvage`).
