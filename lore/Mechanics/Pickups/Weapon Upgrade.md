# Weapon Upgrade

Raises the current weapon's damage by its value, up to the weapon level cap.

`value` is the amount restored (or damage added, or trade value); `lifetime` is how many seconds it stays on the ground. Drop chances live on each enemy's note (`drop_weapon_upgrade`).
